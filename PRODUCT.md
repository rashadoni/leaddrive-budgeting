# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finance operators, holding CFO users, and admin users work inside BudgetPro to import client workbooks, validate financial data, monitor indicators, and prepare demo or client-facing risk views. They are usually in a task flow, not browsing a marketing surface.

## Product Purpose

BudgetPro turns uploaded financial and operational workbooks into scoped company data, reconciled statements, computed risk indicators, audit trails, and terminal-style decision views. Success means imports are easy to run, hard to corrupt, simple to reverse safely, and visibly tied to the indicators they change.

## Brand Personality

Precise, controlled, operational. The product should feel like a finance command surface: dense enough for repeated expert use, explicit about destructive actions, and calm under high-stakes data workflows.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS landing-page patterns inside the app, hidden destructive actions, ambiguous delete wording, generic "AI magic" claims without reconciliation proof, and separate cleanup flows that bypass audit or recompute safety.

## Design Principles

- Put risky data lifecycle actions near the workflow that needs them, but keep confirmation and audit gates intact.
- Prefer preview-before-write for destructive or irreversible changes.
- Reuse proven backend cleanup paths instead of creating parallel deletion mechanisms.
- Make scope visible at all times: company, year, data type, rows affected, and reversibility.
- Keep product UI compact, familiar, and state-rich rather than decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use standard form controls, keyboard-reachable actions, visible focus states, explicit disabled/loading/error states, and copy that does not rely on color alone for risk severity.
